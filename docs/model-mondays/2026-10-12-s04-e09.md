## Overview

Speech models let applications understand and generate spoken language, powering transcription, real-time voice interaction, and accessible conversational interfaces. This session explores building, testing, monitoring, and optimizing a voice agent in the Foundry playground, alongside speech-to-text, text-to-speech, and speech-to-speech models in Microsoft Foundry, including new MAI models.
